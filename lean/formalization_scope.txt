Formalization scope and statement correspondence

The combined project checks the original adjacency-degree constructions and
the direct extension to several real symmetric generators. Its original 189
proof sources and the extension's 25 proof sources are preserved byte for byte
from their separately checked versions. CombinedAudit.lean imports both audit
roots and scans their full project declaration environment, including private
helpers. It does not replace either mathematical proof with an assumption.

The table source-to-statement.tsv identifies the original endpoints and the
extension endpoints. multi_symmetric_coverage.json lists the 15 required
extension theorems. The successful build prints their explicit Lean types.
Checking these types against the written mathematics is a separate review
task; kernel acceptance alone does not establish fidelity to English prose.

Several-generator hypotheses

The parameters satisfy s >= 1 and 1 <= n <= N. H,A_1,...,A_s are real
symmetric n by n matrices. Each A_i has spectral diameter at most N-1;
this is not a norm bound or a bound on the location of its spectrum.
There is no spectral bound on H. The prescribed seed is arbitrary, including
zero. No assumption of full cyclicity or independence of Krylov iterates is
made. The cyclic space is the actual span of all generator words applied to
the seed, and the prefix length is its actual finite dimension r.

The finite Lean index type for coordinates is nonempty and has cardinality
at most N. The s generators are indexed by Fin s. The variables of the
integer free associative algebra are indexed by Fin (s+1), with H at index
zero. The polynomial variable is central. These choices implement the same
matrix and noncommutative-polynomial objects as the manuscript.

Rigidity and descent

For distinct real poles a_i, a rational subspace invariant under
partial_t + H + sum_i B_i/(t-a_i) descends to a real subspace preserved
separately by H and all B_i when r*sum_i diam Spec(B_i) < 1.
The audit derives a constant normalized frame from actual invariance; it
does not assume constancy, a descended frame, or the desired spanning result.
The proof includes the primitive exterior-vector construction, residue
identities, comparison of the highest coefficients, self-adjoint cancellation,
the exterior spectral bound, and real descent. Rank zero is included.

Recurrence, evaluation, and spanning

kappa = s*N^2, a_i = i-1, D = product_i(t-a_i), and
Q_i = product_{j != i}(t-a_j). The literal recurrence is

F_0 = 1,
F_(k+1) = kappa*D*F_k' + kappa*D*x_0*F_k
          + sum_i Q_i*x_i*F_k - k*kappa*D'*F_k.

The formalization connects its integer free-algebra coefficients to matrix
evaluation, coefficient vectors, and the cleared differential Krylov iterates.
The first r rows span exactly the actual joint cyclic space. If r=0 this is
the empty family. All N rows therefore suffice. No rank or spanning witness
is supplied as an additional hypothesis to the public spectral theorems.

The family has N+s*N*(N-1)/2 indexed outputs; zeros and repeated polynomials
still count as indexed outputs. Its coefficients lie in the integer span of
noncommutative words of length at most k in row k, hence at most N-1 overall.
This is a semantic word-degree assertion, not only a syntactic cost counter.
For s=1 every coefficient equals the original two-generator recurrence.

Circuit resources

The audited implementation is a finite staged shared arithmetic circuit.
Its operations and outputs compute the recurrence over any ring, and hence
over matrices of the original input order. It uses no division and shares
intermediate coefficients. Synthetic division is by monic linear factors.
The executed stages, stored outputs, counted gates, and used integer scalars
are tied to the same implementation. The shared zero and identity registers
are counted. It is not a certified exporter of a separate flat bytecode format.

The gate bound, including inputs and the two shared constant registers, is
s+3+30*(s+1)^2*N^2. Every used scalar c satisfies
abs(c) <= 2*(s+1)*(N+1)^3 and
natAbs(c).size+1 <= 3+size(s+1)+3*size(N+1).
This implies the weaker constant-bit bound stated in the manuscript.
These are bounds on prescribed integer constants, not on arbitrary evaluated
real matrix entries.

Limits and other proof inputs

The optional unbounded-spectrum homogenization is not included. Substituting
s=0 into kappa=s*N^2 is not claimed; the ordinary one-generator Krylov family
is a separate observation. The elementary fixed-word lower bound in Remark
1.4 is proved in the paper and is not part of this Lean formalization.

The original tree classification uses the formalized principal-moment
reconstruction theorem of Lu and Li, Theorem 5.4, through PrincipalMomentAudit.
The formal full-representation appendix reconstructs via that theorem and the
all-one polynomial; the written appendix uses McKay and Newton identities.
The original source-to-statement mapping records these proof dependencies.

The only permitted transitive axioms are propext, Classical.choice, and
Quot.sound. Project-local axioms and sorryAx are rejected. Finite exact tests
supplement these universal proofs and do not substitute for them or prove
universal equality between separately implemented emitters.
